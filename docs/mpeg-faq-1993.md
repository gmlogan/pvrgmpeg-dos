# Usenet MPEG-FAQ excerpt (1993)

The entry below is a short excerpt from Frank Gadegast's "MPEG-FAQ:
multimedia compression" — a periodic FAQ posted to `comp.compression` and
mirrored widely across Usenet archives. It is reproduced here only in
part, for identification and historical reference; the full FAQ (all 9
parts, covering the whole MPEG software/hardware landscape circa 1993–96)
is not reproduced here. Read it in full at the archived mirror linked
below.
